ABOUT US
First Canadian O.M Company is a contracting ﬁrm established in 1988, specializing in civil, 
building, and infrastructure projects across Yemen and the Middle East.
The company delivers projects from design to execution, relying on the experience and 
expertise of its engineering and project teams. Every project is executed eﬃciently, on 
time, and in compliance with international standards.
Our core services include:
•
Civil and structural works.
•
Buildings (residential, commercial, institutional).
•
MEP systems (mechanical, electrical, plumbing).
•
Infrastructure (roads, bridges, utilities, water networks, dams, irrigation).
All works are carried out according to project speciﬁcations and international codes, with 
an emphasis on quality, sustainability, and client satisfaction.

First Canadian O.M Company utilizes Building Information Modeling (BIM), a data-oriented 
and intelligent digital representation of facilities, from which various object information 
can be extracted and analyzed according to project requirements.
Throughout the project lifecycle, information is generated and used to support all deci-
sion-making, improving the eﬃciency and quality of project delivery.

porting projects of varying sizes and complexity, including buildings, roads, ports, airports,
